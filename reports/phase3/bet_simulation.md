# Phase 3 - Bet simulation (main leagues, pre-match information only)

Separate from forecast quality. Decision inputs: model (or blend) probabilities and Max PRE odds only; Pinnacle closing is used after the decision, for CLV. Edge = p * Max pre odds - 1 (AH: expected profit from the five outcome probabilities). One bet per match and market (largest edge), flat stake 1, outcomes with Max pre odds above 10 excluded (AH also when the model fair odds exceed it). Only matches with ref_source_<market>_close == pin. Thresholds [0.02, 0.05, 0.1] fixed in advance; ALL are reported, none is selected. Blend bets exist for 1X2 only. CLV = odds taken / Pinnacle closing fair odds - 1; for AH it is defined only when the closing line equals ah_line_pre (n_clv).

**Caveats.** Max odds are the best price across many bookmakers and may not be obtainable at size. Several thresholds, markets and signals are tested, so a single positive cell is not evidence. The sample is conditioned on Pinnacle closing odds being available (an availability gate, not an outcome-based one). The ROI CI is a bootstrap over bets and ignores correlation between bets.
